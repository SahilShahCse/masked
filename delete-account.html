Delete your Masked account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Masked and go to You, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email sahilshah.apps@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Masked account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account, permanently: your profile, posts, comments, poll votes, chats, group messages, reports, blocks and the photos and voice notes you uploaded.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Uploaded files are cleared from our servers within an hour.
