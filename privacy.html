Masked

Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the Masked app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Masked is built so that the people you talk to never learn who you are unless you tell them.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email. Used only to sign you in and keep your account yours. It is never shown to anyone in the app.
  • What you put in your profile. A nickname, and if you choose: a photo, your company, your role and a short bio. Your company is hidden on your posts unless you choose to show it.
  • Your birthday. Asked once to confirm you are 18 or older. It is never shown to anyone.
  • What you post and send. Posts, comments, poll votes, chat and group messages, and the photos and voice notes you attach.
  • Safety records. The reports you file and the people you block.
  • Basic activity. When you were last active, shown only to the people you are chatting with. If you turn on notifications, a device token so we can deliver them.

How anonymity works

  • An anonymous post or comment shows no name, photo or company to anyone. Our server still records who wrote it, so that deleting your account deletes it, blocks work, and people who break the rules can be removed.
  • Poll results show counts only. Nobody can see who picked what.
  • Blocking someone never tells you or them who the other person is.

What we do not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with anyone for their own use.
  • We only disclose data if the law requires us to.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored on servers we operate, and travels to and from the app encrypted (HTTPS). Only we can access it.

Deleting your data

You can delete any post, comment or message you made. Deleting your account (Settings, then Delete account) permanently removes your account and everything tied to it: your profile, posts, comments, votes, chats and the files you uploaded. Files are cleared from our servers within an hour. You can also ask us by email, see Delete your account.

Age

Masked is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn of it, we delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date above. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
