Terms and community rules

Effective 30 October 2026

By using Masked you agree to these terms. Masked is for saying what work is really like. Keep it about work, not about hurting people.

The rules

  1. No names, no doxxing. Never post a name, phone number, email, address or photo, yours or anyone else's. Talk about the job, not the person.
  2. No harassment, threats or hate. Nothing aimed at anyone's religion, caste, gender, race, sexuality, disability, or anything else they are.
  3. Your experience, not made-up claims. Say what happened to you. Do not invent accusations about people or companies.
  4. Nothing sexual, violent or illegal. And no spam, ads or selling.

How we enforce them

Anything several people report is hidden straight away and reviewed. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and people who break them repeatedly lose their account. You can report any post, comment or person from the dots menu, and block anyone so neither of you sees the other again.

Your content

You are responsible for what you post. You keep the rights to it; you let us store and show it in the app for as long as you keep it there. Delete it and it is gone.

Who can use Masked

You must be 18 or older. One account per person.

No warranty

Masked is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and safe but cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. Posts are the views of the people who wrote them, not ours.
