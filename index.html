Masked

Say what work is really like.

Masked is a place to talk about your job without putting your name on it. Post anonymously, run a poll, comment, and chat one to one or in groups with people who get it.

Anonymous by default. Posts go up without your name, and your company stays hidden unless you choose to show it.

Your sign-in stays private. You sign in with Google so the account is yours, but your Google name and email are never shown to anyone.

Kept civil. No names, no doxxing, no harassment. Report or block anyone, any time.
