Masked

Child safety standards

Effective 31 October 2026

Masked is made by Sahil Shah in India. It is an app for adults to talk about work anonymously. These are our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Zero tolerance

Masked does not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children: CSAM in any form (photos, videos, drawings, text or links), grooming, sexualising minors, sextortion, or trafficking. This applies to posts, comments, photos, voice notes, chats, groups and profiles.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Masked. Everyone confirms their birthday when they join, and accounts that say they are under 18 are refused.

Reporting

Anyone can report a post, comment or person from the dots menu in the app, and block anyone so neither of you sees the other again. You can also write to us at sahilshah.apps@gmail.com with "Child safety" in the subject.

What we do

  • Reported content about the safety of a child is reviewed as a priority.
  • CSAM and any content that exploits a child is removed, and the account that posted it is closed.
  • We report CSAM to the authorities: in India through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). We keep what the law requires us to keep for an investigation.
  • We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and Information Technology Act.

Contact
